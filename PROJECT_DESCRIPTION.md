### **What is Ideole?**

**Ideole** is a "stress-test" platform for ideas. Think of it as a high-stakes sandbox where startup founders, researchers, and corporate teams take a raw concept and put it through a professional gauntlet before spending a single cent on building it.

It’s designed to stop people from wasting years on ideas that don't have a market, while giving the "diamonds in the rough" a data-backed path to getting funded.

---

### **How it Works (The Flow)**

1. **The Drop:** You post your idea. You decide who sees it—the whole world (**Public**), a vetted group (**Protected**), or just your internal team (**Private**).
2. **The Scoring:** Instead of shallow "likes," your idea is graded by experts or community members on criterias you specify like 
    * **Originality:** Is this actually new, or just a clone?
    * **Feasibility:** Can we actually build this with today’s tech?
    * **Impact:** If this works, will it actually move the needle?
    * **+ Review:** Review comment/text 
3. **The Pivot:** You get a **Live Dashboard** that aggregates these scores. If your "Feasibility" is tanking, you don't quit—you jump into the **Threaded Conversations**, listen to the critics, and refine the idea in real-time until the scores climb.
4. **The Goal:** Once your idea is "battle-tested" and the data looks green, you use that validation to bring on **Willing Collaborators** or apply for **Funding** with actual proof that your concept holds water.

---

### **The Special Sauce (The Logic)**

* **The "Janitor" Rule:** To keep the platform from becoming a graveyard of dead projects, any community that stays silent for **90 days** is automatically flagged. If no one speaks up, the system dissolves it. It keeps the ecosystem fresh.
* **The Global Access:** If you’re part of a big **Organisation** (like a university or a tech hub), you can jump between different niche communities under that one umbrella, using your professional credentials to build a reputation as a top-tier reviewer.
* **Credibility First:** Every user can link their **LinkedIn** or **GitHub**. When someone tells you your idea won't work, you can check their profile to see if they actually know what they’re talking about.

---

### **Who is it for?**

* **Founders:** To vet their "next big thing" before pitching to investors.
* **Researchers:** To get peer reviews on proposals before they go to a board.
* **Product Teams:** To test new features before they hit the development roadmap.
* **Innovation Hubs:** To manage hundreds of internal ideas without the clutter.
